# Capability

The initiative needs the enduring ability to preserve accepted goals, rationale, constraints, and product understanding; distinguish current authority from historical material; challenge knowledge that may be stale; and expose disagreement rather than silently resolving it.

The founder initially owns this capability. If external adoption develops, accountability and contribution governance will require a separate decision.

# Boundaries

This is an organizational ability, not a particular application, repository structure, specification language, synchronization mechanism, or AI feature.

# Failure condition

The capability fails when apparently authoritative knowledge can become stale without a visible signal, or when maintaining trust requires more effort than the resulting context saves.
