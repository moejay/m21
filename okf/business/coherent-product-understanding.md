# Desired outcome

A builder or team can understand why the product exists, what it promises, how it is experienced, how it is built, and what a proposed change may affect without reconstructing that context from scattered tools and conversations.

# MVP evidence

By the end of a focused session, a user can leave with:

- A structured business and product definition
- Connected product-design and architecture knowledge
- Explicit decisions, constraints, and risks
- Visible dependencies and unresolved impact
- AI-identified gaps and recommendations
- Exportable documents and diagrams derived from the graph

The more important outcome is a coherent mental model, not the quantity of generated artifacts.
