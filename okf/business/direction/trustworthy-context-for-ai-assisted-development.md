# Purpose

Help software maintainers preserve trustworthy product intent, rationale, and constraints throughout AI-assisted software development.

The mission responds to the growing ability of people and coding agents to make changes quickly without guaranteeing that they share the context needed to make the right changes.

# Boundaries

The mission concerns continuity and trust in product knowledge. It does not require a particular specification format, development process, AI model, product feature, or technical architecture.

The initial case is deliberately narrow: one founder maintaining multiple lower-risk software products. Value for wider teams remains an assumption to test rather than an established market claim.
