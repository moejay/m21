# Desired future

Small software teams can return to any product, understand what it does and why, anticipate the consequences of change, and evolve it safely without reconstructing context from scattered or misleading artifacts.

Delivered behavior remains aligned with accepted intent. Relevant goals, rationale, and constraints remain understandable as the software changes, and uncertainty or disagreement in that knowledge is visible rather than silently hidden.

# Boundaries

The vision does not imply that all knowledge must live in one tool or that development can become perfectly predictable. It seeks materially better continuity, understanding, and control while preserving visible uncertainty.
