# Segment

The intended broader segment is small software-product teams where founders, product practitioners, and engineers use coding agents across product development and maintenance.

These teams may experience context fragmentation intensely because people perform overlapping roles, knowledge spans several tools, and generated changes can outpace shared understanding.

# Reach and evidence

The founder-maintainer is the only directly established initial beneficiary. Broader pain, access, willingness to adopt, and willingness to pay have not been researched. This segment is therefore an expansion hypothesis rather than evidence of a market.

# Boundaries

The initial experiment does not target safety-critical, heavily regulated, or high-risk software. Larger organizations and teams not using coding agents remain outside the first decision horizon.
