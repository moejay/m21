# Measure

Compare the effort required to keep product knowledge trustworthy with the context recovery, correction work, uncertainty, and risk that the knowledge avoids.

The assessment includes authoring and updating knowledge, resolving disagreement, identifying staleness, and reviewing generated changes. It also considers whether architectural consequences become clearer and delivered behavior more consistently matches intent.

# Decision threshold

The experiment is not viable when upkeep is comparable to or greater than the benefit, or when outcomes are only marginally better than the current practice. Exact numerical precision is not required for the initial low-risk personal decision.
