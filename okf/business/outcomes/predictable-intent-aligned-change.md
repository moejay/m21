# Desired changed condition

Maintainers can understand the intent and relevant constraints governing a proposed change, anticipate material product and architectural consequences, and produce delivered behavior that more consistently matches accepted intent.

The improvement should reduce corrections caused by already-known context being unavailable. Normal learning, changed goals, and deliberate experimentation remain expected and are not treated as avoidable rework.

# Time horizon and comparison

The initial comparison is qualitative and uses the founder-maintainer's current practice of asking coding agents to infer context from repository artifacts and other scattered sources.

# Guardrail

Greater predictability must not come from hiding uncertainty or treating a stale specification as unquestionable authority.
