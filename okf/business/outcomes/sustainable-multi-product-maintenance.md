# Desired changed condition

After leaving a product untouched, the founder-maintainer can return and regain enough understanding to explain its purpose and feature set, determine how and why it works, identify relevant constraints, and safely scope a change.

This ability must hold across multiple owned products rather than only during continuous work on one product.

# Value

The outcome creates value by reducing context-recovery effort, uncertainty, and corrections caused by missed known context. It should allow limited founder attention to support a broader software portfolio without making the products progressively less understandable.

# Guardrail

The work required to preserve product knowledge must remain lower than the effort and risk it avoids.
