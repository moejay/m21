# Stakeholder context

The initial stakeholder is the project's creator: an independent founder who performs founder, product, and engineering responsibilities across multiple lower-risk software products.

The stakeholder is the first beneficiary, decision maker, evaluator, and bearer of the experiment's time cost. Success in this personal portfolio is sufficient to justify the initial experiment without external adoption.

# Goals

The founder-maintainer wants to return to a product after time away, understand its purpose and feature set, determine how and why it works as it does, assess the consequences of change, and maintain it with less uncertainty and avoidable correction.

# Evidence boundary

This stakeholder is directly observed. Broader archetypes and Personas are not yet justified by research.
