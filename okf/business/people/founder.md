# Role

The Founder decides whether continued investment is worthwhile and bears the opportunity cost of experimentation. The role needs confidence that development effort remains aligned with Business intent and produces outcomes valuable enough to justify its cost.

# Boundaries

This is a functional role rather than a Persona or a claim that all founders share the same circumstances. In the initial experiment, the founder-maintainer performs this role personally.
