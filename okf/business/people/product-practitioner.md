# Role

The Product Practitioner articulates goals, records consequential rationale, and evaluates whether changes produce the intended behavior. The role needs product meaning to remain connected as decisions and software evolve.

# Boundaries

This role describes a function, not a job title or evidence-based Persona. In a small team it may be performed by a founder, engineer, dedicated product professional, or several people together.
