# Need

A maintainer returning to a product needs to regain enough understanding to explain what the product does, why it behaves as it does, and what must remain true before attempting a change.

The understanding must cover relevant goals, feature behavior, decision rationale, constraints, and likely consequences without requiring a complete reconstruction from scattered conversations, documents, tickets, and source code.

# Boundaries

This need is solution-neutral. It does not require one document, a particular specification format, automated code generation, or the elimination of all investigation.
