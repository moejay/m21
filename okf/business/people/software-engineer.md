# Role

The Software Engineer assesses and implements changes while preserving intended behavior and acceptable software quality. The role needs relevant goals, rationale, constraints, and consequences to be available when decisions are made.

# Boundaries

This role does not prescribe a development method or imply that coding agents independently bear accountability. People and organizations remain accountable for accepting and operating generated work.
