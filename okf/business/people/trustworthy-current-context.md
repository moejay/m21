# Need

Maintainers need to know which goals, rationale, constraints, and decisions are accepted for current work. Historical material may remain available, but it must not silently present itself as current authority.

When accepted knowledge and implemented software disagree, the disagreement needs to be visible rather than automatically or silently resolved. Uncertainty must remain explicit so that neither people nor coding agents receive false confidence.

# Boundaries

Trustworthy does not mean infallible or permanently synchronized. It means that authority, uncertainty, and material disagreement can be recognized before knowledge is relied upon.
