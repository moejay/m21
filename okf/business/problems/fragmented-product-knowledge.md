# Problem

Individuals and teams reconstruct product context from disconnected documents, design tools, diagrams, tickets, repositories, and conversations. Decisions lose rationale, artifacts drift, and each discipline develops a different model of the same product.

# Consequences

- Slow onboarding and repeated discovery
- Contradictory product, design, and engineering assumptions
- Generated documents that become stale immediately
- Expensive coordination and avoidable rework
- AI tools producing isolated output without shared context
