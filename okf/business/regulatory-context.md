# Current position

M21 has no accepted claim that a specific industry regulation governs the MVP. The local-first architecture reduces hosted data custody but does not remove privacy, accessibility, intellectual-property, or AI-governance responsibilities.

# Questions requiring future decisions

- What project knowledge may be sent to configured AI providers?
- What consent, retention, and redaction controls are required?
- Which markets or customers introduce privacy or sector-specific obligations?
- What accessibility conformance evidence is required for release?
- How are AI proposals, provenance, and human acceptance recorded for governance?

This concept records uncertainty rather than inventing compliance requirements.
