# Risk

Creating, reviewing, updating, and reconciling product knowledge adds work. If this effort duplicates existing sources or requires constant manual synchronization, it may move rather than solve the maintenance burden.

# Consequence

The founder could spend more time maintaining the account of the product than understanding and changing the product itself. Teams may also abandon upkeep, increasing the stale-specification risk.

# Evaluation

Compare knowledge upkeep against reduced context recovery, avoided corrections, clearer consequences, and improved intent alignment across multiple products.

# Stop implication

Stop the experiment when its net benefit is only marginal, negative, or dependent on upkeep that is unlikely to continue.
