# Risk

The initial evidence and evaluation come from one founder who performs founder, product, and engineering roles across several owned products. The products, working style, tolerance for upkeep, and judgment of improvement may not represent other teams.

# Consequence

Personal success can justify continued personal investment but cannot establish a broad market, general adoption, willingness to pay, or suitability for other risk profiles.

# Response

Treat small AI-assisted software teams as an expansion hypothesis. Broader claims require external observation of real work, alternatives, adoption friction, maintenance behavior, and value.
