# Risk

A specification or other accepted source may cease to agree with current intent or implemented software while continuing to appear authoritative. People and coding agents may then act confidently on incorrect context and compound the resulting mistakes.

# Exposure

The founder considers stale specifications the most serious risk in the Business case. Incorrect authority may produce worse outcomes than explicitly missing context because it suppresses the signal that investigation is needed.

# Required response

Current accepted knowledge must be distinguishable from historical material. Material disagreement between accepted knowledge and implemented software must be visible rather than silently resolved.

# Stop implication

The experiment should stop or change direction if it cannot expose likely staleness and conflict without excessive manual maintenance.
