# Actions

- **Primary:** one dominant action in a local decision context.
- **Secondary:** available but not visually dominant.
- **Quiet:** low-emphasis navigation or disclosure.
- **Disabled:** unavailable with an adjacent explanation when the reason is not obvious.

Every action needs a visible focus state, a clear verb, sufficient target size, and a non-color indication of disabled or pending state.
