# Character and feel

The product should feel like a **focused working studio** shared by experienced collaborators.

It is intelligent without theatrical AI styling, technical without resembling an infrastructure dashboard, and structured without becoming bureaucratic.

## Desired qualities

- Calm under complexity
- Precise but not sterile
- Confident without pretending certainty
- Dense when useful, progressively disclosed when not
- Human-directed even when AI is active
- Durable enough for long product-development sessions

## Avoid

- Glowing AI gradients and anthropomorphic assistant decoration
- Excessive glass, blur, or motion
- Color used without semantic purpose
- Dashboard tiles that fragment a coherent document
- Hidden automation or irreversible generation
