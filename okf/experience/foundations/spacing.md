# Spacing system

Spacing communicates hierarchy. Related controls remain compact, document content breathes, and groups are separated by stronger intervals than items within a group.

The system supports dense product knowledge without sacrificing target size, scanability, or readable separation. Density is never achieved by hiding labels or collapsing required context.
