# Entry

The builder creates a project and describes an idea in their own words.

# Flow

1. M21 creates a minimal project and shows what is currently known.
2. The agent identifies the highest-value uncertainty and asks one focused question.
3. The builder answers, redirects, or selects a concept to develop directly.
4. The agent proposes concepts and typed relationships.
5. The builder reviews and accepts, edits, rejects, or defers the proposal.
6. M21 persists accepted knowledge and updates validation.
7. The builder chooses another suggested direction or works elsewhere in the graph.

# Success

The builder leaves with a coherent model and explicit open questions, not merely a generated requirements document.
