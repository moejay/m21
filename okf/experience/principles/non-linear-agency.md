# Principle

The workspace offers paths, not gates. A user can start from an idea, screen, journey, capability, decision, or architecture concept and move laterally or vertically as understanding develops.

# Implications

- No layer is locked behind completion of another layer.
- Suggested next steps are ranked recommendations, not required wizard stages.
- Incomplete knowledge is represented explicitly.
- Dependency and impact information make freedom safe rather than removing freedom.
