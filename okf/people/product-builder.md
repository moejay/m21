# Context

The product builder may be a founder, product-minded engineer, designer, architect, or technical lead. They have an idea or an evolving product but do not always have specialists available for every decision.

# Needs

- Turn incomplete thinking into a structured model without completing a rigid questionnaire
- Understand trade-offs instead of receiving unexplained generated answers
- Move freely between business, product, design, and architecture
- Recognize missing concepts and risky assumptions
- Preserve rationale so later decisions retain context
- Produce useful artifacts without maintaining duplicate documents
