# Context

Product strategists, designers, engineers, architects, and operators often work concurrently at different depths of the same product. Their tools and artifacts hide dependencies, causing stale assumptions and expensive handoffs.

# Needs

- Own and review different regions of the product graph
- See why another discipline's change may affect their work
- Avoid broad alerts for changes that cannot affect their contract
- Review proposed impact before canonical knowledge changes
- Understand history, rationale, and unresolved conflicts
- Collaborate through portable, version-controlled project knowledge

Real-time multiplayer editing and general project management are not required for the MVP.
