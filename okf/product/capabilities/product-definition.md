# User outcome

A user can build a traceable explanation of why the product should exist, who it serves, what outcomes it promises, and which capabilities are necessary.

# MVP knowledge

- Vision and mission
- Business goals and success measures
- Personas, users, and stakeholders
- Problems, needs, and desired outcomes
- Product capabilities and boundaries
- Policies, assumptions, constraints, and risks
- Relationships from goals through capabilities to design and architecture

# Acceptance outcomes

- Every core capability traces to a user need or business outcome.
- Unsupported assumptions and missing success measures are visible.
- Product changes can identify potentially affected design and engineering knowledge.
- The product definition can evolve after design or architecture work has begun.
