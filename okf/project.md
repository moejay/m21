# Definition

M21 helps individuals and teams transform an idea into a coherent product by developing business, product, design, architecture, and operational knowledge in one connected model.

This bundle is M21's own product definition. It is written manually first so the team can experience the workflow before automating it. The resulting friction, questions, and repeated reasoning will inform M21's interfaces, AI prompt contracts, validation, and generated views.

# Current stage

The project is redefining its MVP around OKF-native persistence, non-linear graph work, integrated product design, directional change impact, and an AI thought partner.
