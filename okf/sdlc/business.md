# Purpose

Establish the business context that gives every later product and engineering choice meaning.

# Defines

- Vision, mission, and desired business outcomes
- Personas, stakeholders, needs, and problems
- Business capabilities and operating responsibilities
- Success measures and evidence
- Policies, regulations, compliance obligations, and business constraints
- Assumptions, risks, and explicit boundaries

# Agent posture

Ask why the problem matters, distinguish evidence from assumption, challenge unsupported goals, identify affected stakeholders and regulation, and avoid prematurely prescribing product or technical solutions.

# Downstream influence

Business changes may affect Product, Visual Design, System Design, Architecture, and every deeper realization. Deeper internal changes do not redefine Business unless they expose a contradiction or new evidence.
